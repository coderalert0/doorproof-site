Privacy policy

Effective November 7, 2026. Doorproof is an app for checking IDs at the door of a business. This policy covers the Doorproof app for iPhone and iPad.

What the app reads

Doorproof uses the camera to read the barcode on the back of an ID, a passport's machine-readable lines, or (when staff tap Check front) the printed front of an ID. All of this is read on the device. No image is saved, and nothing read is sent anywhere.

What is kept on the device

  • Always: tonight's counts (admitted, turned away, looked closer, fakes caught) and, for pass-back alerts, a keyed hash of each card scanned tonight. The hash is made with a key created each night and deleted at the venue's closing time, and can't be turned back into a card or a person.
  • With records on (Doorproof Pro, off by default): each scan's time, result, age bracket, issuing state, the checks that raised a question, the staff and door names entered on the device, whether the guest was admitted, and a keyed hash of the card. Names and birth dates are kept only if the venue turns that on. Records are deleted after the number of days the venue chooses (7 by default). They leave the device only if the venue shares an export.
  • Ban and VIP lists (Pro): the names, birth dates and notes the venue enters. They stay on the device.
  • Settings: the venue type, age and other preferences.

What is shared: Fake Watch

Fake Watch shares something only when staff mark a card as fake, or mark a card Fake Watch doubted as real. What it shares: a signature describing how that card's barcode was made (its layout and print settings), whether it was fake or real, an anonymous venue identifier (a hash of a random value made when the app is installed), the city and state the venue entered (optional), the two-letter code of the issuing state, and the time. These reports are stored in Apple's CloudKit public database, where other Doorproof users' apps read them, and are dropped after 180 days. They contain no name, ID number, birth date, address or photo. Fake Watch sharing can be turned off in Settings.

Mobile IDs

Mobile IDs are checked through Apple's ID Verifier. Apple's own system screen shows staff the guest's name, photo and age result; Doorproof learns only whether staff approved, and keeps nothing from the ID.

What is not collected

No account, no advertising, no tracking, no analytics, no data sold or shared with third parties. Purchases and subscriptions are handled by Apple; Doorproof receives only whether a subscription is active.

Venues' responsibility

A venue decides whether to keep records and for how long, and is responsible for following its local law. Some states limit scanning IDs or keeping their data.
